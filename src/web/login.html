Manage your Linux splitter from any computer on your local network.

Closing your browser leaves the splitter running.

LINUX MANAGEMENT

Sign in to your splitter

Use the username and password configured on your radio.

Connecting to service…

Use the access code from the private setup file on your Linux server. The service shows the file’s location when it starts. After setup, sign in with the radio credentials.

Your credentials are sent securely to this Linux server. They are not stored in this browser by the splitter.

About the browser’s certificate notice

This local service may use its own certificate. If your browser asks, check that the address belongs to your Linux server before trusting it. Your administrator can also install a certificate your browser already trusts.
